Where to start

You do not need to have it all worked out.

Plenty of people ring us holding a plan they have read four times and still find confusing. That is normal. The NDIS uses its own language, and the gap between a funded support category and what actually happens on a Tuesday morning is wide.

So we start with your week rather than your plan. What is hard at the moment? What would you be doing if it were not? From there we can tell you which supports your funding covers, what it does not, and whether we are the right provider for it.

  • No cost and no obligation for the first conversation
  • We will say plainly if another provider suits you better
  • Everything confirmed in writing before support starts
